Kataclix metricas

Kataclix metricas is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Kataclix and Causario. It is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), it uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload the channel's own videos, set their title, description, thumbnail and publishing time;
  • post comments as the channel on its own videos;
  • read the channel's own statistics (YouTube Analytics) to decide what videos to make next.

It does not access any other user's data. Privacy policy · YouTube Terms of Service
